# Personal Assistive Intelligence (PAI)

## Sequence: **1/22**
- Utterance: Personal Assistive Intelligence (PAI) is an architecture that combines general-purpose AI models, goal-directed agents, operational harnesses, and evolving personal context to extend an individual’s ability to reason, learn, decide, and act. It carries relevant understanding across interactions, incorporating the individual’s knowledge, preferences, objectives, constraints, and decision-making principles into how it interprets requests and supports action. Its personal core, portability, and networkability make it a persistent, adaptive layer of intelligence centred on the individual. It uses personal context to shape reasoning and execution, so recommendations reflect the user’s objectives and judgment and actions respect the user’s constraints and authority boundaries. It improves by selectively capturing meaningful experience from interaction, preserving its rationale, and applying it where appropriate. A networked PAI helps individuals discover, evaluate, adopt, and adapt capabilities developed by others. Its personal dimension establishes relevance, portability preserves and extends it, and networkability adds capabilities and experience the individual has not developed alone. Transferred context needs personal interpretation, shared expertise needs adaptation, and both require a harness to manage context, permissions, execution, and feedback. The individual contributes intent, expertise, experience, and judgment. Models and agents contribute reasoning, synthesis, planning, and action. The harness connects them through memory, tools, orchestration, and oversight. The architecture’s usefulness depends on how the components work together to help individuals understand situations, make decisions, and accomplish meaningful goals while preserving their judgment and control. It aims for a continuing relationship between human judgment and machine capability and requires meaningful memory, selective transfer, trustworthy knowledge exchange, and clear user control.
