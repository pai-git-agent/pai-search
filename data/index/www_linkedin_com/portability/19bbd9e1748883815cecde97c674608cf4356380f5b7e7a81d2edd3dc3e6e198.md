# Portability

## Sequence: **4/19**
- Utterance: Portability lets accumulated context and judgment travel across tasks, domains, tools, and service platforms. Across domains, it brings relevant knowledge, preferences, methods, and decision principles to unfamiliar problems. Across platforms, it maintains continuity as users change models, applications, or providers. A preference from one setting is a potentially useful signal whose relevance must be assessed. Successful transfer distinguishes enduring judgment from local habits and situational exceptions. Cross-platform portability requires a receiving system to understand and use transferred context. It depends on exporting structured knowledge, preferences, decision principles, and experience, interpreting them consistently across systems, and preserving the rationale, scope, and conditions for application. A reliable-supplier preference is more useful when it specifies when reliability takes priority, what evidence supports assessments, and which exceptions the user accepts. Portability makes context a durable resource whose value can persist as the technology changes.
