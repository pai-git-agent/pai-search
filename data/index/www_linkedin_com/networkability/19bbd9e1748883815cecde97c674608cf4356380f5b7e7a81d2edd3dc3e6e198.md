# Networkability

## Sequence: **5/19**
- Utterance: Networkability connects an individual to people, agents, shared expertise, knowledge sources, reusable workflows, and collective experience. Expertise is distributed, so individuals can draw on methods developed elsewhere and a personal system can access specialised knowledge beyond its user’s experience. Networked PAI helps people discover, evaluate, adopt, and adapt others’ capabilities. With appropriate authorisation and validation, selected refinements can be contributed back. Shared expertise expands individual capability and validated individual contributions improve resources available to others. A network can spread unreliable assumptions and unsafe practices as readily as useful expertise. It needs clear permission and privacy boundaries on what can be shared, with whom, and for what purposes, traceable authorship, sources, versions, and modifications, and evaluation suited to the intended use and consequences. Reusable methods must remain separate from private execution data. A shared procurement workflow should preserve the privacy of supplier contracts, pricing, and internal priorities. Adoption should confer no automatic access or authority, and requested permissions and actions remain subject to individual control.
