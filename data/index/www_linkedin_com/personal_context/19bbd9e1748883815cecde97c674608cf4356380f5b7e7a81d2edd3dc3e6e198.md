# Personal context

## Sequence: **6/22**
- Utterance: Personal context includes domain expertise, accumulated experience, preferences, priorities, constraints, and personal definitions of success that shape which alternatives people consider, how they assess trade-offs, and which outcomes they accept. Personalisation can evolve through capture, retrieval, and application without retraining the underlying model, provided the system identifies what matters, when it stays valid, and where it applies. Repeated interactions develop context through instructions that establish objectives, preferences, constraints, and expectations, corrections that expose differences between system assumptions and user intent, and explanations that convey the reasoning and trade-offs behind decisions. A record of a vendor choice preserves an event, while the reason for that choice can capture a decision principle. Useful context preserves both the preference and its scope, such as when reliability takes precedence for critical supplies and when price matters more for routine purchases. As preferences and circumstances change and earlier interpretations prove incorrect, personal context should remain inspectable, correctable, and under user control.
