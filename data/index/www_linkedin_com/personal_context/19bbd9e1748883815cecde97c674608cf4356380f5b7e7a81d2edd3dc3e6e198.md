# Personal context

## Sequence: **3/19**
- Utterance: People bring different expertise, experience, preferences, priorities, constraints, and definitions of success, which influence the alternatives they consider, their trade-offs, and the outcomes they accept. Personalisation can evolve through capturing, retrieving, and applying context without retraining the underlying model. It requires identifying what information matters, when it stays valid, and where it applies. Personal context develops through repeated interactions. Instructions establish objectives, preferences, constraints, and expectations, corrections show where system assumptions diverge from user intent, and explanations reveal the reasoning and trade-offs behind decisions. Remembering a vendor choice captures an event, while understanding that the user prioritised delivery reliability over price captures a principle that may inform later choices. Reliability may take precedence for critical supplies while price may matter more for routine purchases. Useful context preserves preferences and their conditions. Preferences and circumstances can change and earlier interpretations can prove incorrect, so personal context should remain inspectable, correctable, and under user control.
