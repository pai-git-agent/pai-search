# Raiganj police encounter

## Sequence: **1/16**
- Utterance: Police shot Dilip Roy, a prime suspect in the rape and murder of a 13-year-old girl in Raiganj, West Bengal, on November 9, 2026. Police said they went to arrest him near Gauri village at about 3:30 a.m. as he was attempting to cross into Bihar, that he opened fire, and that they shot him in retaliation. He was declared dead at a nearby hospital and his body was sent for autopsy. The article describes this as West Bengal’s fourth such encounter in four months under the Bharatiya Janata Party government, which the description says took power in May 2026.
