# Indian Olympic Association

## Sequence: **2/19**
- Utterance: The Indian Olympic Association (IOA) held elections for its 2026–30 leadership. Under its constitution, a candidate for IOA president must be a Sportsperson of Merit (SOM) or a General Assembly member. In 2022, P. T. Usha became the IOA’s first woman president after being elected unopposed through the SOM route. Eight newly elected SOMs will join the General Assembly and be eligible to vote and stand in the December 5 Executive Council polls. One male and one female SOM will also join the Executive Council. Sathish Sivalingam alleged that IOA resources were being used in an organised effort to influence the electoral roll in favour of a contesting candidate who was also IOA president, without naming Usha.
