# Sportspersons of Merit election

## Sequence: **3/19**
- Utterance: The November 9, 2026 election returned four women Sportspersons of Merit (SOM) from six candidates. The winners were Saina Nehwal, Anju Bobby George, Dola Banerjee, and Rani Rampal, and P. T. Usha was unsuccessful. The electoral roll for the four female representatives consisted of 80 SOMs, 40 men and 40 women. On November 7, 2026, Gagan Narang, Vijender Singh, Yogeshwar Dutt, and Kamlesh Mehta were elected unopposed as the four male SOMs after eight other candidates withdrew. The eight elected SOMs will join the IOA General Assembly, making them eligible to vote and stand in the December 5 Executive Council polls, and one man and one woman will join the Executive Council. Usha and Sathish Sivalingam filed separate complaints with the Electoral Officer about alleged vote solicitation and electoral influence.
