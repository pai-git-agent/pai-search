# P. T. Usha

## Sequence: **1/19**
- Utterance: Indian Olympic Association (IOA) President and track-and-field athlete P. T. Usha failed to win a Sportsperson of Merit (SOM) seat in the November 9, 2026 election. She could stand in the December 5 Executive Council polls only if a National Sports Federation nominates her to the General Assembly. Such a nomination was considered unlikely, and she was not expected to be one of the two Athletics Federation of India representatives. In 2022, she was elected unopposed as the IOA’s first woman president through the SOM route. She complained to Electoral Officer Sanjay Kumar Srivasatava that former archer Dola Banerjee violated the Model Code of Conduct by circulating a WhatsApp message soliciting votes. Usha objected to a message sent through an account bearing Banerjee’s name that sought support for the New Bharat Panel and asked voters to elect four named candidates, giving their ballot serial and roll numbers.
