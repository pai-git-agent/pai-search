# Sheikh Hasina

## Sequence: **1/15**
- Utterance: Sheikh Hasina is the deposed Prime Minister of Bangladesh. She fled to India in August 2024 after an uprising toppled her government, and her passport was revoked soon afterward. Her legal team sought a new passport, and a source in Dhaka told The Hindu she requested a new passport and National Identity Card through a London-based law firm. Bangladesh’s High Commission press secretary said authorities were unsure whether the request was genuine because the firm’s email included no proof of authorisation. Acting Foreign Minister Humayun Kabir said Hasina had fled after committing crimes, described her as a terrorist, said she had been sentenced to death, and alleged she and several Awami League colleagues were responsible for violent crimes and financial fraud. Bangladesh is seeking her return, and India had yet to announce a decision on handing her over. Some Awami League leaders said on social media that preparations for her return were underway.
